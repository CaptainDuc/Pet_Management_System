# Internal workspaces

Mode: Operate. Audience: administrators, owners, reception staff and veterinarians.
The user requests the approved light customer/login identity on all four internal
dashboards and direct dashboard entry after login, including saved sessions.
Customer login continues returning to the landing page.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One familiar Pet Service workspace, with each role's daily work one step
away. Remove the duplicated authentication toolbar and dark legacy shell.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit navy #273172, sky #49B3F4, peach #FFBC7D, white and pale-blue
grounds, Fira Sans/Nunito Sans, soft borders and rounded controls. Use Lucide icons.

STORY: Sign in, enter the role's dashboard, identify the current task and move
between existing business screens. Role switching remains limited to assigned roles.

FIRST VIEWPORT: White brand/user header; a white task sidebar on desktop; a clear
page heading and spacious work surface. On mobile, an accessible menu reveals all
destinations and logout; tables scroll inside their own bounds.

FORM: Extend the already approved portal shell and vocabulary to the internal
routes; reuse the established surface concept, seed:aced74861d2e5570. Preserve
existing API actions and indicate inherited demonstration content where present.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
